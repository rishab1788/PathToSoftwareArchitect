Is it polite to say "I disagree"?
Saying "I disagree" directly can be perceived as harsh in many professional and cross-cultural contexts. Instead, phrases like "I’m not sure I fully agree" and "I don’t quite see it that way" are preferred, even when there is complete disagreement. In the next lesson, you will learn how to soften your disagreement.
