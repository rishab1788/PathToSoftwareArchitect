Real-life situation: Business idioms in action
 You'll hear some colleagues use idioms often. Others rarely do. You don't need to use them in every conversation. Only use them when you are sure that the other person will understand.

Think of idioms as spices. 🧂 🌶️  A little bit adds flavor, but too much will spoil your meal.

See eye to eye
Meaning: To agree with someone on something. If you don't see eye to eye, you don't agree with them.


Find common ground
Meaning: To find a point of agreement or shared understanding.


You hit the nail on the head
Meaning: You are absolutely right. I totally agree with you.


Meet halfway
Meaning: To compromise, to each give a little and reach an agreement.


On the same page
Meaning: To understand or agree with someone on the matter in a similar way.


Speak the same language
Meaning: To have a similar perspective; to understand each other well.
